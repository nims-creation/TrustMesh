Transfer Money
Simulate the full offline payment flow: Encrypt → Inject → Gossip → Bridge Upload → Settle. Each step mirrors the real BLE mesh protocol.
1. Encrypt & Inject
Payment is AES-256-GCM encrypted with RSA-wrapped key, then injected into the sender's phone.
2. Gossip Round
Offline phones share packets with neighbors via Bluetooth. The more rounds, the further the packet travels through the mesh.
3. Bridge Upload
Bridge nodes with internet access upload the packet to the backend. Exercises idempotency — only 1 packet should settle.
